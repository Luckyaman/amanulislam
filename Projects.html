I have contributed to various research and development projects throughout my academic and professional career. Below is a list of selected projects showcasing my expertise and interests:

  • Anomaly Detection in 5G/6G Networks
    Conducted research as a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs, focusing on anomaly detection using Variational Autoencoders (VAE) and addressing fake base station threats.
  • Anonymous Networking Detection in Cryptocurrency
    Investigated methods to detect anonymous networking behaviors in cryptocurrency transactions to enhance network security.
  • Graphical Authentication Techniques
    Developed a novel graphical authentication method to prevent shoulder-surfing attacks using digraph substitution rules and shifting conditions as part of my Master’s research at the University of Malaya.
  • Systematic Mapping Study
    Conducted a systematic mapping study on tools to identify emotions and personality traits during my tenure as a Research Assistant at the University of Malaya.
  • Network Security System Design
    Designed a network security system using IP Tables during my undergraduate studies at The Millennium University, Bangladesh.
