Review Recognition Achievements

In December 2023, I received a Certificate of Reviewing from Computers in Human Behavior, an Elsevier journal. This recognition highlights my contribution to the peer-review process by evaluating a submitted manuscript, reinforcing my dedication to academic excellence and scientific integrity.

In July 2025, I was honored with a Certificate of Reviewing from Neurocomputing, an Elsevier journal. This acknowledgment reflects my active role in supporting the peer-review system and upholding the standards of high-quality research within the field.

In July 2025, I received a Certificate of Reviewing from Knowledge-Based Systems, an Elsevier journal. This award acknowledges my contribution in reviewing a research paper, reflecting my engagement with the academic peer-review process and my commitment to maintaining research quality.

In June 2025, I was recognized by the prestigious Elsevier journal Computers & Security for reviewing a manuscript. This certificate highlights my active participation in cybersecurity research and my service as a reviewer for top-tier journals.
